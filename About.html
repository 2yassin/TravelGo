Our Story

Born From a Passion for Adventure

TravelGo started in 2018 with a simple idea: travel should be easy, affordable, and unforgettable. What began as a small team of three travel enthusiasts has grown into a global platform serving thousands of travelers every year.

We believe that every journey — whether it's a weekend escape or a once-in-a-lifetime adventure — deserves to be planned with care. That's why we've built a platform that combines the best destinations, curated packages, and expert local knowledge, all in one place.

Today, TravelGo connects travelers with over 150+ destinations across 60+ countries, with a team of passionate experts who live and breathe travel.
