Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we collect and protect your data.

Table of Contents

1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Your Info 3. Cookies & Tracking 4. Data Security 5. Your Rights 6. Contact Us
Last updated: October 2026

1 Information We Collect

When you use TravelGo, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and billing details provided during account creation or booking.
  • Travel Details: Passport information, travel dates, and destination preferences required for booking.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited on our site.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Process your bookings and send confirmation emails.
  • Communicate with you about your trip or respond to inquiries.
  • Send promotional offers (only if you opted in).
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

3 Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies help us remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and analyze site traffic.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features of the site may not function properly.

4 Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. We use industry-standard encryption (SSL) to protect your personal information during transmission and storage.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access, update, or delete your information.
  • The right to opt-out of marketing communications.
  • The right to request a copy of the data we hold about you.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@travelgo.com.
